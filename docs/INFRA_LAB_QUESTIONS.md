# Infra Sim question design

The current bank has 25 questions, four choices per question, and a separate
explanation for each selected choice. Distractors use relevant mistakes: confusing
an SSID with isolation, access with tagged membership, aggregate with per-client
limits, or a passive panel with active switching. Several choices propose a useful
check that still leaves part of the stated requirement untested.

The intended DOK mix progresses from recognizing functions to applying concepts
and evaluating investigation or acceptance plans. DOK describes cognitive demand,
rather than length, vocabulary, or how difficult a distractor looks. The design
uses [Karin Hess's guide to Webb's Depth of Knowledge](https://portal.ct.gov/-/media/sde/mbl/documents/webbs_depth_of_knowledge.pdf?rev=7f7c8f58f0e4434da9eaea22c6318ded)
as a reference. The levels below are authoring judgments about the reasoning the
items require, rather than an external assessment certification.

| Lab | Question 1 | Question 2 | Question 3 | Question 4 | Question 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1: Home | 1: WAN role | 2: Placement and comparable measurements | 2: DHCP fault isolation | 2: Airtime tradeoff | 2: Limits of ping evidence |
| 2: Office | 1: Passive panel circuit | 2: Fiber data versus PoE | 2: Repair a particular circuit | 2: Gateway/subnet correction | 2: Explain changed UPS load |
| 3: Classroom | 1: Access VLAN function | 2: Shared uplink membership | 2: Classify untagged ingress | 2: VM endpoint configuration | 3: Evaluate a controlled repair plan |
| 4: Staff/guest | 2: Segmentation and policy | 2: Channel width overlap | 2: Per-client versus aggregate limits | 2: Rule order and verification | 3: Evaluate a complete acceptance plan |
| 5: Two wings | 2: Distribution architecture | 2: Distance and power constraints | 3: Discriminate competing fault hypotheses | 2: Preserve a working segment during repair | 3: Evaluate policy and power handover evidence |

The four strategic items ask students to combine observations, preserve explicit
constraints, and select a plan whose tests distinguish causes or demonstrate the
whole required outcome. A familiar term inside a longer question does not itself
make that question strategic. Teachers can extend these items with an oral or
written justification during class discussion.

## Saved attempts

Question bank 1 is frozen for pre-update attempts, including their original
three-choice order. Bank 2 uses four choices and an order derived from the lab,
question, and attempt identity. Reopening an attempt preserves its order; a new
attempt can have a different order. Correct choices and explanation keys remain
server-side until that question is answered.

New attempts and teacher previews use the current bank. Existing attempts retain
their question version, selected answers, explanations, and scoring. Repeats and
teacher resets use the current bank after clearing the attempt's answers. When
revising published content again, add a new bank version and keep prior banks
unchanged.

## Teacher catalog

The Infra Sim Labs section lists every lab for a teacher, including teachers
without a class. The class selector changes student-access indicators, rather
than restricting teacher previews. An assigned lab whose class Infra Sim grant
is off is identified separately. The school-wide Infra Sim control still applies.
Class lists come from current ownership on the server; student assignment and
membership checks continue to apply to every student lab request.
